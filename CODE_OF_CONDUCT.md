# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want Codex Project Hygiene to be a welcoming, practical, and safety-minded
open-source project. Participate with empathy, assume good intent, discuss ideas
rather than people, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds
and levels of experience.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- sharing synthetic reproductions instead of private user data;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting boundaries when someone asks a conversation to stop.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained personal attacks are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security reporting form](SECURITY.md) and label the report as a code-of-conduct
matter. Do not include unrelated secrets or personal data. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when reasonably
necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and willingness to
repair harm. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, but this
volunteer project cannot promise a response time or a particular outcome.

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone is acting on behalf of Codex Project Hygiene.
